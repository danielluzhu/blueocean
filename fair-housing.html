Fair Housing

Fair Housing & Equal Opportunity

Blue Ocean Housing is committed to fair housing practices for every applicant and resident at 4544 20th Ave NE, Seattle, WA 98105.

⚠ Please note:

This page is a general informational summary, not legal advice, and is not a substitute for review by a qualified attorney or the agencies linked below. Fair housing laws and local ordinances change — please confirm current requirements before relying on this page for compliance purposes.

⌂

Equal Housing Opportunity We are pledged to the letter and spirit of U.S. policy for the achievement of equal housing opportunity throughout the nation. We encourage and support an affirmative advertising and marketing program in which there are no barriers to obtaining housing because of race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, or national origin.

Our Commitment

Blue Ocean Housing does not discriminate against any applicant or resident on the basis of any characteristic protected under federal, Washington State, or City of Seattle law. Housing decisions — including applications, screening, leasing, and terms of tenancy — are made on a consistent, non-discriminatory basis.

Federal Law — the Fair Housing Act

The federal Fair Housing Act (42 U.S.C. § 3601 et seq.) prohibits housing discrimination based on:

  • Race or color
  • National origin
  • Religion
  • Sex (including, per current HUD guidance, sexual orientation and gender identity)
  • Familial status (including families with children under 18, and pregnant people)
  • Disability

Washington State Law

The Washington Law Against Discrimination (RCW 49.60, including RCW 49.60.222) extends protections further. Categories commonly cited under state law include:

  • Race, creed, color, and national origin
  • Sex and marital status
  • Sexual orientation and gender identity
  • Families with children status
  • Honorably discharged veteran or military status
  • Disability — including sensory, mental, or physical disability, and the use of a trained dog guide or service animal
  • Source of income — including Section 8 and other housing vouchers or subsidies

State law is amended periodically; confirm the current text of RCW 49.60 with the Washington State Human Rights Commission before relying on this summary.

City of Seattle

4544 20th Ave NE is within Seattle city limits, so the property is governed by the City of Seattle's Open Housing ordinance (Seattle Municipal Code Chapter 14.08), which is broader than state and federal law. In addition to the categories above, Seattle law generally also prohibits discrimination based on:

  • Ancestry and political ideology
  • Age
  • Parental status
  • Gender expression
  • Immigration or citizenship status
  • Student status
  • Participation in a Section 8 or other subsidy program
  • Criminal record — Seattle's Fair Chance Housing Ordinance (SMC 14.09) separately restricts how criminal history may be used in tenant screening and advertising

Seattle's fair housing and tenant screening ordinances are updated from time to time. Current requirements — including any tenant screening, advertising, and notice obligations — are maintained by the Seattle Office for Civil Rights.

A note on "Seattle / King County"

Because this property sits within Seattle city limits, Seattle's municipal code — not the King County Code, which applies to unincorporated areas of the county — governs fair housing obligations here.

Reasonable Accommodations

Applicants and residents with disabilities may request reasonable accommodations or modifications related to an application, lease terms, or use of the property. To make a request, contact us at reach.seattle.homes@gmail.com.

Filing a Complaint

If you believe you have experienced housing discrimination, you can contact:

  • U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) — Office of Fair Housing and Equal Opportunity
  • Washington State Human Rights Commission
  • Seattle Office for Civil Rights
